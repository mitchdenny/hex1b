Source: <https://static.poly.pizza/6f3cef79-867c-4b3c-9d97-346e465e4079.glb>

Upstream SHA-256: `08894cd4be8a628420657ad5c7fbceaa75a658450c33a757018c06a99c6fb4ae`

The source page lists 1,056 triangles; the downloaded GLB contains 1,088.
It is a low-poly military helicopter, not a specific
Apache/attack-helicopter replica. This sample derives a stylized gunship from it:
rotor triangles are separated, transforms are baked, coordinates are normalized
and reoriented, and material base colors are converted from linear to sRGB.
The sample adds its own simple stub wings and decorative pods at runtime.
The source creator does not endorse these modifications or this project.

`prepare-model.py` reproduces `Helicopter.mesh` from the hash-verified source.
Converted SHA-256: `2fa5851d814f87c11b8e066c3e06c0ddea6f9cb8689e22573d5ac3a10c0acaa2`.

The binary contains only colored triangle positions, rotor pivots, and part
identifiers. No textures, animation tracks, or source metadata are included.
Redistributions of the derived mesh must retain this attribution and license link.
